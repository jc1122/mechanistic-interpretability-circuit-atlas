[inherited task-source catalogue](#inherited-task-source-catalogue), or the [method-to-source table](#methods-used-by-the-atlas).
Each record identifies its source type and public link; the linked entry explains the comparison and its limits.

The earlier catalogue records were checked against arXiv, Crossref, proceedings or publisher metadata; venues and author lists of
elhage2021, bricken2023, chan2022, nanda2023, chughtai2023, charton2023, frankle2020, weiss2021 and pythonchess were re-checked on
2026-11-08. Entry-source audits reconciled the later records on 2026-11-13 using primary or registry metadata and the original public
software/forum sources. These are identity checks, not independent full-text reviews or verification of the atlas's scientific claims.
Earlier reading limits, including abstract-only comparisons, remain in the entries and records below. Publication-practice reading
scope is recorded in its own section. A key is a link label: its year need not equal the printed year (zhang2024 names the 2023 work
of Zhang & Nanda). Long author lists may use three authors plus "et al."; incomplete inherited attributions are labelled, not guessed.
