# Symbolic differentiation (chain and product rule)

> - **Task.** Write the unsimplified derivative of a prefix expression built from x^1 to x^8, sin, cos, exp, + and *. Example (in words): for sin of x^2 the answer is cos of x^2 times the derivative of x^2, written out without simplification.
> - **Hand-built net.** Answer only: a 15-layer net (16 heads + 1,963 neurons) propagates derivative lengths and expands a template before copying source spans. Scratchpad: a 5-layer net (6 heads + 1,100 neurons) writes one rewrite row per source node; exact on every tested expression.
> - **Trained nets.** Trained from scratch, both formats solve: answer only 3/3, scratchpad 3/3 and 10/10 in the final cell. Training only the last emitter MLP on the frozen hand-built parser fails in all 12 seed-runs (.46–.92): it bypasses the frozen reference interface. No trained net extrapolates to depth 3 or powers 7–8.
> - **Verdict.** Solved (from scratch: scratchpad 10/10, answer only 3/3; frozen-parser setup 0/12). Closed form: partial (first-layer rank-4 token-pair table keeps exact 1 in 16/16 nets; no compact form for the rewrite stage).
> - **Links.** [Weights](https://huggingface.co/jc1122/circuit-atlas-nets/tree/main/chain-rule-differentiation) · Reproduce: not available yet · [Terms](../GLOSSARY.md)

## Task and status
**Task.** Differentiate a prefix expression over x^1..x^8, sin, cos, exp, + and * without simplification. Prompt: BOS, 0–7 inert pad dots, the source symbols, `=`; vocabulary 104. Format **A** (answer only): the expanded derivative (≤ 157 symbols at depth 3). Format **T** (scratchpad): a bottom-up register rewrite, one 16-token row per source node (≤ 15 rows); source references name original subtrees, derivative references name earlier rows, and expanding the last register gives A. Training domain: operator depth 0–2, powers 1–6, padding 0–3; two operator-edge compositions (sin→cos, exp→product) are withheld. Split: SHA-256 of the canonical expression, 80/20; 256 held-out expressions per seed. Exact = whole teacher-forced output including the end token `$`.
**Formats.**
- **A**: answer only (the expanded derivative).
- **T**: scratchpad; a bottom-up register rewrite, one 16-token row per source node.

**Status.** 2026-11-08. HAND exact (A and T). TRAIN: A and T three-seed screens, T ten-seed final cell (10/10). PART: A and T, first run and a data retry (3 seeds each), all failed. Standard diagnostics cover every cell, best and final snapshots; a front-end substitution covers all 16 TRAIN nets. Ten-seed finals of TRAIN A and the PART cells are not run.

## Summary
A formula-built transformer (HAND) differentiates every tested expression (A 16 heads + 1,963 neurons, T 6 + 1,100). Trained from scratch (TRAIN), both formats solve: answer only (A) 3/3 seeds, scratchpad (T) 3/3 and **10/10 in the final cell**, all exact on held-out expressions. Training only the last emitter MLP on the frozen hand-built parser (PART) fails in all 12 seed-runs (.46–.92), also with 5.6x more distinct training expressions: the learned emitters bypass the frozen reference interface. One circuit type per TRAIN cell. Their first layer is a content-weighted prefix count: a rank-4 token-pair attention table replaces it with exact 1.0 kept in 16/16 TRAIN nets, while HAND's uniform average gives T 0–.38. The rewrite stage behind it has no compact closed form. No trained net extrapolates to depth 3 or powers 7–8.
## Setup
See [METHODS.md](../METHODS.md) for the shared conventions. AdamW lr 1e-3, wd .1, warmup 1000, cosine 30k, clip 1, batch 16 fresh expressions per step (fresh pad 0–3), eval every 500 steps, checkpoints every 2500; best snapshot = highest held-out exact; stop at all-seed exact 1.0 or on a 5k plateau after 22.5k. PART fp32, TRAIN TF32 updates, all evaluations fp32. All seeds of a cell in one process on consumer GPUs; analysis on CPU. Data retry (PART r2): depths 0–3 and powers 1–8 in training, 58.9k–59.3k distinct expressions per seed at 22.5k steps vs 10.5k–10.6k in r1. Extension sets, 128 rows per seed: depth 3, powers 7–8, the withheld compositions, padding 4–7.
Networks: [huggingface.co/jc1122/circuit-atlas-nets/tree/main/chain-rule-differentiation](https://huggingface.co/jc1122/circuit-atlas-nets/tree/main/chain-rule-differentiation) (28 nets + 2 HAND; best snapshots; each run's 256-row held-out batch in `test_<run>`). ## Architecture as used
GPT-NeoX, 2 heads per layer (d_head 192), d_model 384, MLP 384 ReLU, no LayerNorm, no positional encoding (causal mask only), untied readout, vocabulary 104, context 289. **A: 15 layers; T: 5 layers.** TRAIN learns every tensor of its format's shape. PART: HAND frozen; re-initialised and trained only the stage MLP, A layer 13 (last descent emitter), T layer 3 (local rewrite MLP): `dense_h_to_4h` [384, 384] + bias, `dense_4h_to_h` [384, 384] + bias; the stage input is masked to the HAND channels that MLP uses (T 33, A 75; absolute frame inputs removed, logical subtree addresses remain). Files: `hand_{A,T}`, `part_{A,T}_{r1,range_r2}_s{0-2}` (4 tensors; insert into `hand_<fmt>`), `train_{A,T}_r1_s{0-2}`, `train_T_final10_s{0-9}`, `config.json` (task, vocabulary, HF configs) and `manifest.json` (seed, rung, best step, recorded and re-scored held-out exact, final exact, sha256).
## Prediction before training
Recorded before training (3-seed screens). PART A and T ≥ 2/3 within 10k steps: **broken** (0/3 each, also after the data retry). TRAIN T ≥ 2/3 within 30k: **held** (3/3, then 10/10). TRAIN A ≤ 1/3: **broken** (3/3). T TRAIN poor depth transfer: **held** (0 at depth 3 in every seed). T's final emitter a rule/slot/child-reference lookup: partly held (its last MLP is reproduced by a table over exactly these keys, but the table is not compact). At least one solved seed with decodable spans but no HAND-like depth-match head: held (the first layer is a weighted count, not a depth match).
## Hand-built reference
A uniform prefix head gives bracket balance, completion counts and a position frame; depth/recency-routed heads and child reads give each subtree's start, end and children. T then fetches the completed node and its children's results and emits the local rule (power rule, sin/cos/exp, sum, product) as one 16-token row. A instead propagates derivative lengths and descends an expansion template before copying source spans; no lookup table of expressions. Bricks: one-hot rows, step thresholds on scalars, attention reads; address score −24(p−a)², reference gate 64 (a gate of 1024 displaced one depth-3 product-tree address by > .5), output gain 30. Exact on 1,497/1,497 expressions (all 160 of depth ≤ 1, stratified above, powers 1–8, padding 0–7) and 2,265/2,265 on the widened retry domain; minimum logit margins 30 (A) and 27.5 (T). Sizes are the formula allocation, not a measured minimum.

The emitter must hit unit-precise addresses on frozen thresholds; more data (r2) did not supply them. ## Closed form
- **TRAIN A and T, first-layer attention (all 16 nets, best):** a_ij = softmax_{j≤i} S_r[t_i, t_j], S = (E W_Q + b_Q)(E W_K + b_K)ᵀ/√192, S_r its rank-r SVD truncation (a 104 × 104 token-pair table per head, weights only); values and the rest of the net unchanged. Equivalently each head returns Σ_u n_u exp(S[t,u]) v(u) / Σ_u n_u exp(S[t,u]), with n_u the count of token u in the causal prefix: a content-weighted histogram. Whole-output exact on 256 held-out rows per net: native 1 → rank 4, 8, 16 and full table 1 in **16/16** (2·4·104 = 832 numbers per head); rank 2 in 12/16, rank 1 in 7/16 (A 3/3, T 4/13). HAND's uniform average (S = 0) instead: T 0–.38, A .81 / 1 / .93. Accepted: the T front end is a content-weighted count of rank ≤ 4, not HAND's average; A's is near-uniform. Seed 2 (T r1): with MLP0 as a_j = ReLU(α[t,j] + Σ_h C_h β[h,:,j]) also substituted, 99.2% (native 99.2% on that cohort); a rank-8 front plus all MLP0 hinges uses 205,440 numbers vs 887,040 native and keeps 1 → 1 on fixed-shape batches in all three r1 seeds.
- **TRAIN T, last MLP (MLP4), r1 seeds 0–2, best and final:** m̂(q) = b_out + W_out θ[s(q)], s = (slot, rule, node and child endpoints, power, tree depth, line, current token), θ = mean training activation per state (609 states): 512/512 fresh held-out expressions in all six nets (global-mean control 0/512), but 234,240 fitted numbers vs 295,680 MLP parameters and 5/256 on mixed-shape rows: **rejected** (no compression, fixed shape only). Its write alone is compact: m̂ = μ + a[y]·B over the 53 emitted tokens y (rank 24, 10,872 numbers) is exact on two mixed-shape cohorts in 6/6 nets, but only when y is supplied by the hand-built parse; a linear readout of y from the MLP4 input gives .87–.98 (derivative addresses off by ±1), so the token decision has no compact form.
- **PART T, stage MLP (best and final, seeds 0–2):** a_j = ReLU(b_j + u_j·FOP + v_j·SLOT + f_j FP + g_j FE1 + h_j FE2 + e_j END), output c + Σ_j w_j a_j on the symbol block only, inputs = HAND rule, slot and node/child endpoint coordinates. 12/12 comparisons keep the native accuracy with zero changed tokens (128 fresh rows and 256 guard rows .770–.875, native errors included); 53,096 fitted numbers (18% of the MLP). Faithful to the failing nets, not a solution.
- Whole-program fits on pruned TRAIN T circuits fail (14 joint fits: 11.3%; refit 56.6%).
## Weights excerpt and reading
PART T seed 0, best, neuron 138 of the fitted stage form: ReLU(b + w_op·FOP + w_slot·SLOT − .172 FP − .0025 FE1 − .0068 FE2 − .111 END) (fit coefficients, rounded). It is an address boundary on the node end, shifted by rule and slot, not a power detector; deleting the neuron from the native net drops exact .852 → .547. HAND layer-3 emitter (T, formula weights): a text neuron is ReLU(OP_i + SLOT_j − END − 1) with weight 1 into its output symbol, so it fires only for its exact (operator, slot); a reference neuron is ReLU(ptr + 64(OP_i + SLOT_j − END) − 128), which passes the child address ptr (an integer scalar FE1, FE2 or FP) through unchanged when both gates are on and is 0 otherwise; layer 4 turns the address into the reference token with step thresholds. The trained PART emitter replaces these gates by graded hinges on the same coordinates, which is why it misses unit-precise addresses. TRAIN T MLP4 (r1 seed 0, best; vote = W_U·W_out column, a weight slice): syntax and coefficient tokens have dedicated detector neurons (`:` n381 vote +1.88, runner-up +.33), address neurons are broad bumps that also vote for the neighbouring address (`d7` n18 +1.22, `d8` +1.14): an address is the overlap of several bumps. TRAIN first-layer tables are computed from E, W_Q, W_K, W_V of the archived nets (formula above).
## Comparison
Same task, opposite outcome to the arithmetic entries: there, PART on frozen hand-built parts is the easy rung; here the frozen parser hands the emitter discrete addresses that a ReLU MLP trained by gradient does not place, while full TRAIN learns its own interface and solves. Trained T nets keep HAND's variables decodable (final seed 4: bracket depth and completion R² ≥ .99, child endpoints ≥ .94, REF .96) but compute them with weighted-count heads instead of HAND's depth-match heads. Scratchpad vs answer only: both formats solve TRAIN at similar steps (A 16–17.5k, T 14.5–19k); T is smaller by construction and shares subexpressions, so this is not a compute-matched format test. The scratchpad follows [Nye et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.00114); there is no delayed generalisation of the [Power et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.02177) kind with fresh data every step.
## Controls and limits
Length (trainer extension evals, fp32, 128 rows per seed; best / final): every TRAIN net scores 0 at depth 3 and at powers 7–8 (those outputs need tokens never emitted in training); withheld compositions A 1, T r1 1 / .48 / 1, T final .47–1 (6/10 best and 5/10 final at 1); padding 4–7 A .79–1, T 0–.09. PART on the same sets: A depth 3 0 (r1), T depth 3 .29–.39 (r1). HAND 100% everywhere. Size (best, cumulative ablation with constant replacement): TRAIN T seed 2 9 heads + 793 neurons, seed 1 9 + 941 (native 10 + 1,920, HAND 6 + 1,100), both at 99.2% on the discovery cohort but 93.4% on a guard set: local upper bounds, not minima. The diagnostic cohort is one fixed shape; edges, alignment and interpolation were run on part of the nets (interpolation on one pair). Probe R², nf = 0 interchange and uniform-head divergence ties were not taken as use or absence. Pending: ten-seed finals of TRAIN A and the PART cells, public code.
